S³: Unified Zero-Shot Singing Synthesis and Conversion via Joint Lyrics–Melody Transcription

S³ transcribes source audio with supplied lyrics into a symbolic score, then generates singing conditioned on a reference recording.

How to use

  1. Listen. Select a song and reference. Compare the S³ output with the source vocals or original mix.
  2. Inspect. Use the piano roll to view predicted notes and lyrics. Copper notes connected by a curve share one syllable (melisma).
  3. Navigate. Click a lyric to seek or a timestamp to play a phrase. Enable MIDI overlay to hear the predicted notes alongside the audio.
